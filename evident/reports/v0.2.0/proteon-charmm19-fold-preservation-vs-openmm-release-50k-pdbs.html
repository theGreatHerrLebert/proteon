Claim

Across 50 000 PDBs sampled (seed=42) from the wwPDB random sample, proteon's CHARMM19+EEF1 minimization preserves fold within 0.005 TM-score of OpenMM CHARMM36+OBC2 minimization on the same inputs. Both runners use a 100-step L-BFGS-style minimizer at effective NoCutoff. The 1k version of this claim shipped in v0.1.4 with median(openmm − proteon) tm_diff = +0.0040 (proteon CHARMM19 median TM=0.9944, OpenMM CHARMM36+OBC2 median TM=0.9991); this 50K extension confirms the agreement holds at full corpus scale where sample-noise effects cannot mask population-level drifts. First 50K run scheduled; headline numbers will be filled in at lock time.
